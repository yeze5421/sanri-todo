A LITTLE ROOM TO BREATHE

A list should help you breathe.

Three Days is a to-do list with an expiry date. Every task gets just 72 hours from the moment you add it: fresh on day one, fading on day two, and drifting away on day three.

After 72 hours, unfinished tasks disappear with a gentle thought: “Maybe it wasn’t that important.” Finished tasks stay in Done.

No account needed. Your list stays on this device. Time keeps moving while Three Days is closed, and expired tasks are cleared when you return. Removing the saved app data also clears your list.

No streaks. No falling-behind reminders.
Do a little today. Or give yourself a break.
